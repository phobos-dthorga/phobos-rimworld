# VFE Factory additions: research and decisions (28 Oct 2026)

What was read, what was decided and by whom, for the mod `mods/PhobosFactoryAdditions/` (D'thorga Factory
Additions). The owner's intent (28 Oct 2026): a series of modifications to Vanilla Furniture Expanded -
Factory, starting with human meat as a neutroamine feedstock. Dated record; the mod's README and changelog
carry current behaviour.

## How a factory bill works (code read, not tested in game)

Read on 28 Oct 2026 from the decompiled Pipe System (Vanilla Expanded Framework) and VFE Factory assemblies in
the ignored `.local/decompiled/`, and from VFE Factory's installed XML (Workshop item 3686924415).

- A factory bill is a `PipeSystem.ProcessDef`. A building offers the bills listed in its
  `CompProperties_AdvancedResourceProcessor` `processes` list; other mods add to that list with a
  `PatchOperationAdd` on the comp's xpath.
- An ingredient names either one `thing` or a `thingCategory`. A category ingredient accepts anything
  `IsWithinCategory` says is in it, minus the Defs on its `disallowedThingDefs` list (`Process.CheckInputSlots`).
  The owner's earlier finding stands: the factory's own grab ignores the hopper's "allow taking off" state.
- `ticks` is the batch time; `priorityInBillList` orders the Add process menu (stock 0, Kimoju's insect 1,
  our human meat 2, Kimoju's fish 11).
- `considerBuildingCompResource` lets a bill draw a piped resource from the building's own `CompResource`;
  VFE Factory sets it on the stock neutroamine bill only while Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded is active, by
  `PatchOperationFindMod` on the mod's display name.
- `historyEventWhenFinished` can fire an Ideology history event; VFE Factory's "Mince human corpses" fires
  `ButcheredHuman`. Kimoju's mod removes it.

## The stock neutroamine bill already took human meat

`VFEFactory_SynthesizeNeutroamine` takes 20 of the `MeatRaw` category plus 3 chemfuel and gives 5 neutroamine
in 4800 ticks, with no exclusion list. Human meat (`Meat_Human`, generated by the game from the Human race,
meat market value 0.8) is in `MeatRaw`: vanilla meal recipes have to exclude it from the same category by
name. Nothing in VFE Factory filters human meat except the mincer's output. So, by the code, a hopper with
human meat ticked fed the stock bill before this mod existed; only its text ("rendered animal fat") said
otherwise. **Not tested in game.**

## Drug lab recipes as factory bills (28 Oct 2026)

**Scan.** All 300 active mods and the game were parsed the way RimWorld 1.7 loads them (LoadFolders.xml
version blocks with fallback to the newest lower block, ParentName inheritance). A recipe counts if it lists
the drug lab in `recipeUsers`, if a ThingDef `recipeMaker` does, or if a patch adds it to the lab (only
Luciferium Expansion does that). Result: **86 recipes from 17 sources**; VFE Factory automates 10 on its
medicine granulator and LE Ammo one more (FSX, on the refinery); **75 had no factory bill**, and no active
mod adds granulator bills. Two other benches copy the lab's recipes (VFE Production's electric lab, VGE's
compact lab) and need nothing. No DLC adds a drug lab recipe.

**Machine mechanics** (decompiled VFE Factory and Pipe System, `.local/decompiled/`): the granulator's bills
are hand-written `PipeSystem.ProcessDef`s; VFE Factory generates processes only for the autoloom, the
machining bay and the fish farm, from `recipeMaker`, never from RecipeDefs. `researchPrerequisites` hides a
process from the Add menu until done. The comp ticks every 250 ticks. Output: a hopper-only process spawns on
the interaction cell only when a factory hopper whose filter accepts the first result is there, and waits
("output hopper incorrect" or "full") otherwise; **but** with `maxOutputCount` 0 a failed placement still
resets the process (output lost), and with a cap above `stackLimit - count` the `TryAbsorbStack` return is
ignored, so a batch that overfills the stack loses the surplus. Hence the cap rule. Power is the building's
comp only, drawn in full even when idle (`CompPowerTrader_Overclocked`); no per-process power, skill or
count field exists. Stack limits that bind: `DrugBase` 150 (not 400), medicine 25, hemogen pack 10,
vaccines and antiserums 15, pathogen samples 1.

**Stock value rule**, verified on every stock drug bill (flake 250 work -> 4000 ticks for 4; yayo 350 ->
5600; go-juice 600 -> 9600; wake-up 900 -> 14400; medicine 700 -> 11200; penoxycyline and joints batch 1):
ticks = lab work x 4 x batch, inputs x batch.

**Values.** Every bill's inputs, output, ticks and research are in the mod README; source files, read 28 Oct
2026: Medicines+ `1.6/Defs/Drugs/*.xml` (recipeMaker, WorkToMake; `Aniracetam` inherits no bulk recipe or
work skill, `SSalts` has no research); Deep Pills `1.6/Mods/MedicinesPlus/Defs/RecipeDefs/Infusions.xml`
(the x8 recipes; the x1 recipes use a products form that may not load); Biological Warfare
`1.6/Defs/CombatDiseases/<Disease>.xml` with base cost lists in `Bases/` (vaccine: 8 neutroamine, 1
penoxycyline, 25 pathogen, 2600 work; antiserum: 6 neutroamine, 1 glitterworld medicine, 25 pathogen, 1800;
pills: 6 neutroamine, 1 sample, 600; seven diseases, Infection has no operation research); CE
`Defs/Drugs/Psychite_Psycho.xml` (16 leaves, 600 work, no research); Rimatomics `1.7/Defs/Drug/Iodine.xml`
(3 neutroamine, 500, PenoxycylineProduction); Hemofungus `1.4/Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_ExtractHemogen.xml`
(60 -> 4, 1600, Deathrest); Complementary Odyssey `Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_Production.xml` (1 vacdew + 5
steel or vacstone blocks, 600, DrugProduction); Glitterworld Medicine `Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_Production.xml`
(5 synthread, 1 component, 4 plasteel, 1 medicine, 1800, MedicineUltratechProduction); Farmable Neutroamine
`1.6/Defs/ThingDefs_Items/Items_Resource_Manufactured.xml` (5 leaves -> 1, 250, NeutroaleafRefining);
Passive Exoskeletons `1.7/Defs/Recipe/Recipe_materials.xml` (75 chemfuel + 8 plasteel -> 25, 6000; its
x100 recipe already names the refinery as a recipe user, which does nothing on a processor building);
Simple Washing Machine `Defs/RecipeDefs/DetergentRecipes.xml` (25 chemfuel + 1 or 3 neutroamine -> 3, 1500
or 2000, SterileMaterials).

**Deep Pills on 1.7.** Its `loadFolders.xml` has only a v1.6 block, with the Medicines+ payload in an
`IfModActive` subfolder. The owner sees the infusion bills at the drug lab on 1.7 (28 Oct 2026), so the
fallback loads it; our infusion Defs carry `MayRequire="ODs.DeepPills"` inside the Medicines+ folder.

**Not ported, and why:** Luciferium Expansion (owner); Biological Warfare's seven pathogen samples
(1 pathogen -> 1 sample, 200 work: trivial, and samples stack to 1), the disease sampler (a utility item),
flesh-breaker (left out of the scope question; trivial to add); the rumsuit (VFE Pirates) and medic bag
(VAE Accessories), apparel with quality; Deep Pills' x1 infusions (the x8 ones cover them); FSX (LE Ammo
covers it on the refinery).

**Checks.** `scripts/check-factory-patches.py` (added in this change) loads the game and the active mods as
RimWorld would, then checks every patch xpath matches one node, every Def name a ProcessDef uses exists,
every hopper-only bill's cap is within its stack limit, and that defined and added bills match. Run against
the owner's installation on 28 Oct 2026: 299 active mods, 35589 Def names, 45 ProcessDefs, 0 errors. It
found the missing cap on the 0.1.0 human-meat bill. An offline check is not an in-game test.

## Why the 0.3.0 bills never reached the machines (29 Oct 2026)

**Owner's report:** the new bills showed in RimDex but not in any machine's Add process menu.

**Cause, read in RimWorld 1.7's code** (`Verse.ModContentPack.InitLoadFolders` and
`Verse.DirectXmlLoader.XmlAssetsInModFolder`, decompiled from the installed `Assembly-CSharp.dll`; output kept
in the ignored `.local/`): the game walks a mod's load folders from the last one `LoadFolders.xml` lists to the
first and keys every Defs and Patches file by its path **inside its load folder**. The first file to claim a
path loads; every other file with that path is skipped, with no log line. 0.3.0 had seven
`Patches/MedicineGranulator.xml`, two `Patches/BiofuelRefinery.xml`, and a
`Patches/NeutroamineSynthesizer.xml` in both `Common` and `Mods/FarmableNeutroamine`. The bill Defs had
unique file names, so they all loaded (hence RimDex), but only the last folder's patch for each machine ran.

**Effect with the owner's mods:** 4 of 45 bills reached a machine (glitterworld medicine, still hidden by
its research; both detergents; leaf neutroamine). The human-meat bill, its stock-bill exclusion and the
stock bill's corrected text were dropped too; queued human-meat bills kept running because their Def loaded.

**What ruled out the alternatives first:** the owner's Player.log had no patch failure from this mod; Pipe
System's Add menu (1.6 and 1.7 builds, identical here) lists the machine's `processes` and hides only bills
with unfinished research; the save's research covered 25 of the 44 new bills (all of Medicines+, the
infusions, psycho, vacsop, leaf neutroamine, polyamide and the detergents); no other active mod's XML or DLL
replaces the granulator's, synthesizer's or refinery's process list.

**Why the check missed it:** `check-factory-patches.py` loaded every file in every active folder. Both
checks now share `files_the_game_loads` in `check-mod-layout.py`. Run against the 0.3.0 commit's files, the
factory checker reports the 9 skipped patch files and 41 bills on no machine; the layout check refuses a
shared path without needing the game.

**Separate finding, repaired in 0.3.2** (`Mods/VanillaChemfuelExpanded/Patches/VFEFactoryRepair_VanillaChemfuelExpanded.xml`;
`PatchOperationSequence` stopping at its first failed step confirmed in the 1.7 assembly): with Combat Extended, Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded and Vanilla Factions
Expanded - Deserters all active, VFE Factory's own `VanillaChemfuelExpandedPatch.xml` fails at its
armour-piercing shell step, because Combat Extended's VFE Factory patch removes that bill first. A failed step ends
its PatchOperationSequence, so the two steps after it never run: `considerBuildingCompResource` on
`VFEFactory_AstrofuelFromChemfuel`, and the factory-hopper flag on Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded's chemfuel tap.
Seen in the owner's log (29 Oct 2026); the consequence is read from the XML, not seen in game.

## Ideas for later additions (agent suggestions, not decided)

- **Flesh-breaker** (Biological Warfare, 2 neutroamine -> 1, 1000 work) and **Luciferium Expansion's** seven
  products, if the owner changes their mind: same shape as the 0.3.0 bills.
- **Hemogen packs from human corpses** on the automated mincer: pure gameplay (corpse blood is not
  transfusable), so an owner decision and labelled as such.

- **Twisted meat (Anomaly)** as a neutroamine feedstock, with its own bill and yield, the way Kimoju treats
  insect meat. Entity flesh is the one meat players rarely want to eat.
- **Rendered fat as an intermediate item** (tallow): corpses to the mincer, meat to a renderer, tallow to the
  synthesizer, soap (Dubs Bad Hygiene) or candles. A pathways design in the owner's taste; a bigger slice.
- **Toxic wastepacks** from drug synthesis (Biotech): Pipe System has `wastePackToProduce`, so a batch could
  leave a little waste. Honest chemistry, but a nerf, so an owner decision.
- **A 1.7 fallback for Kimoju's bills**: that mod declares 1.6 only. If it breaks on 1.7, this mod could carry
  insect and fish bills of its own, switched on only when Kimoju's mod is absent.
- **A mod setting for the yield** needs C#; not worth a DLL for one number while the value is being tuned.
