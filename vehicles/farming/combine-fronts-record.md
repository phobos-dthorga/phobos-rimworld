# The combine's grain fronts: run and review (20 Nov 2026)

**What was bought.** Two attempts each of the combine harvester's two grain fronts, drawn alone on white, north facing, from the
OpenAI image API (gpt-image-2, medium quality): the standard front, 6 cells wide, at 1536 x 512, and the wide front, 8 cells wide, at
2048 x 704 (OpenAI takes no shape longer than 3 to 1, so its canvas is a little taller than the front). The owner's go of 20 Nov 2026,
two attempts each; dry run first: at most US$0.40; cap US$0.45. Spent: about **US$0.128** (US$0.030 each for the standard, US$0.034
for the wide, from the returned token counts; none an estimate). Request: [combine-fronts.json](combine-fronts.json); design:
[farming batch one](../../../docs/development/farming-batch-one.md).

**Why drawn alone.** A front sits in front of the body, not on it, so it is a part of its own with the body as a style reference (as
the tanker's pressure vessel was), not an edit of a crop of the body (rule 13 covers parts drawn on the body). Each is its own overlay
graphic, wider than the body's 6-unit canvas for the 8-cell front. The widths are the owner's of 20 Nov 2026: 6 and 8, centred on the
4-cell body.

**Reference, our own.** Image 1, the combine's body the owner picked on 19 Nov 2026 (attempt 1). No game or other author's art was
uploaded. The runner's near-copy check flagged none of the four.

**Review (agent, at about native size).** All four are straight down, on white, with no text, crop, combine or wheels, and each has
the planned parts top to bottom: a cutter bar with guards along the front edge, crop dividers at the ends, a yellow reel, a steel-grey
cross auger, a pale floor, and a throat at the middle of the back edge.

| Front | Attempt | What is right | What is wrong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 1 | Full set of parts; a strong throat | The reel's many close bats read a little like a ladder; the dividers are blunt corners |
| Standard | 2 | Pointed dividers outside square end plates; a clean reel with fewer spokes | None found |
| Wide | 1 | The most finished: cone dividers, a reel on four spiders | A different look from both standard fronts |
| Wide | 2 | Angled end walls and wedge dividers, the same family as the standard front's attempt 2 | None found |

**Agent's lean:** standard 2 and wide 2, so the two either/or fronts read as one family.

**Decision (owner, 20 Nov 2026, by question card):** **standard front: keep both**, attempt 2 the default and attempt 1 an alternate
look; **wide front: attempt 2.** Standard 1 and 2 and wide 2 went to `art-masters` (`vehicles/farming/combine-front-standard-1`, `-2`,
`combine-front-wide-2`); wide 1 to `art-references`, not picked.
