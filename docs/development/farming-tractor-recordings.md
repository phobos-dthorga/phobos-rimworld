# Real recordings for the Thane tractors (20 Nov 2026)

**Status (20 Nov 2026):** research done, nothing downloaded or shipped. The owner chose real recordings over generated ones for the
tractors' sounds ([farming batch two](farming-batch-two.md), "Research, sounds, overlays and the install"). Licences allowed: public
domain, CC0 and CC BY, with credit (assets/AGENTS.md); never non-commercial, no-derivatives or a custom licence.

**How it was found.** An agent searched Freesound, Wikimedia Commons, the Internet Archive and US government media on 20 Nov 2026, and
read each licence on the recording's own page unless marked unverified. Freesound hides most results in packs unless searched with
`&g=0`. **Freesound's original files need a signed-in account to download**, which agents never use: the owner downloads those, or we
work from what Commons and the Internet Archive serve openly.

## What exists, by size

| Size | Cover | Best candidates (licence) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small, older (about 60 kW) | **Good** | mickyman5000's Massey start, loop and stop, and a Zetor 6945 start and stop (CC0); kyles' close idles and an old tractor's start, drive and shut-off (CC0); the Finnish broadcaster YLE's archive: a Valmet working with a harrow, start and pull-away under load (1985), a 1963 Fordson Super Dexta ploughing (CC BY 4.0); a John Deere 5820 from the Work With Sounds museum project on Commons (CC BY 4.0) |
| Medium, modern (about 130 kW) | **Partial**: nothing confirmed at 130 kW with a cab | Soundholder's Case IH CX90 rev ramps, shut-off and pass-bys (CC BY 3.0, about 66 kW); lucasfuchs' Fendt from inside the cab (CC0); bruno.auzet's Renault working, recorded with a Schoeps pair (CC0); kyles' "modern" tractor idle, drive-by, stop and shut-off (CC0); jmagiera's MB-trac 1400 idle (CC0, a lossy file) |
| Large articulated (about 400 kW) | **None found** | No openly licensed Steiger, Quadtrac, 9R, Versatile or Big Bud anywhere searched. Weak stand-ins only: a landfill compactor working (CC0, unverified as articulated), a combine working (CC0) |

Implement bonus: two ploughing recordings (CC BY 4.0), a tractor's hydraulic lift (CC0, possibly a landfill machine).

**Licence notes for the owner.** Two CC BY 4.0 uploaders (dibko, klankbeeld) add a request not to use their sounds in royalty-free
stock libraries or to pass on the raw files; a free mod shipping edited files is neither, but it is the owner's call. Several Commons
and Internet Archive items are CC BY-SA (share-alike), the owner's call too. Rejected outright: anything NC or ND, and commercial
libraries (listed in the agent's report, not to be searched again: Motion Array, Soundstripe, AudioJungle, MuseHub, Mixkit,
ElevenLabs, Iron Audio, Shockwave-Sound, asoundeffect; Soundholder's paid CX90 library beyond its three free clips).

**A lead, unverified:** the US auctioneer Purple Wave published some tractor videos on YouTube under CC BY before YouTube ended that
option on 31 Jul 2025; one (a 1988 Ford 6610) is on Commons as CC BY 3.0. They sell many large articulated tractors, so an older video
might give the T400 a real voice, checked one by one.

**Our inference, not a finding:** without a real large articulated tractor, its sound could be built by lowering and layering the
medium recordings, labelled as a simplification.
