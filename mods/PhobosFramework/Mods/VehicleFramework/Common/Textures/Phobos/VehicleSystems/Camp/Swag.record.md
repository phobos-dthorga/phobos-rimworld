# Swag

Made with ComfyUI, local, FLUX.2 Klein 4B base (Black Forest Labs, Apache 2.0) with our private house style model (house-v1, trained on our own picks), on 21 November 2026: **AI-generated**, one try, picked by the owner the same evening. The prompt is in `assets/vehicle-systems/requests/camp-gear-house.json`; the batch report is `assets/vehicle-systems/generated/run-20261121-2142.md`. Only our own shipped art was used as references, for style; no game or other author's art. Exported by `scripts/art/export_sprite.py` at 128 px a cell (128 x 256 for its 1 x 2 footprint), the background cleared with the background AI's mask. The east and south facings are the north one turned by script (90 degrees clockwise and 180 degrees); the game mirrors east for west.
