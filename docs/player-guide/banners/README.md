# The player guide's chapter pictures

The picture at the top of a chapter. Each is a scene made with generative AI on the owner's own graphics card (Flux.1-schnell or
Z-Image, both Apache 2.0), picked by the owner on 28 November 2026 from night 18's tries, and titled by
`scripts/art/make_preview.py`, which adds "D'thorga", the chapter's heading and a line under it in Arial: **no text is generated**.
No game art and no other author's art is in them. The record beside each picture names its model, seed, prompt and hashes;
`assets/phobos-previews/ship_night18_scenes.py` made them, and running it again makes them byte for byte.

Chapters with a mod of their own open with that mod's Workshop picture instead (Factory, Medical, Patches, Radio, Water Tap,
Farming). The lines under the titles are the agent's wording, for the owner to change. The modders' front page and the reference
site share one picture, kept with the site in `docs/reference/images/`.
