# VFE Factory additions: research and decisions (28 Oct 2026)

What was read, what was decided and by whom, for the mod `mods/PhobosFactoryAdditions/` (D'thorga Factory
Additions). The owner's intent (28 Oct 2026): a series of modifications to Vanilla Furniture Expanded -
Factory, starting with human meat as a neutroamine feedstock. Dated record; the mod's README and changelog
carry current behaviour.

## How a factory bill works (code read, not tested in game)

Read on 28 Oct 2026 from the decompiled Pipe System (Vanilla Expanded Framework) and VFE Factory assemblies in
the ignored `.local/decompiled/`, and from VFE Factory's installed XML (Workshop item 3686924415).

- A factory bill is a `PipeSystem.ProcessDef`. A building offers the bills listed in its
  `CompProperties_AdvancedResourceProcessor` `processes` list; other mods add to that list with a
  `PatchOperationAdd` on the comp's xpath.
- An ingredient names either one `thing` or a `thingCategory`. A category ingredient accepts anything
  `IsWithinCategory` says is in it, minus the Defs on its `disallowedThingDefs` list (`Process.CheckInputSlots`).
  The owner's earlier finding stands: the factory's own grab ignores the hopper's "allow taking off" state.
- `ticks` is the batch time; `priorityInBillList` orders the Add process menu (stock 0, Kimoju's insect 1,
  our human meat 2, Kimoju's fish 11).
- `considerBuildingCompResource` lets a bill draw a piped resource from the building's own `CompResource`;
  VFE Factory sets it on the stock neutroamine bill only while Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded is active, by
  `PatchOperationFindMod` on the mod's display name.
- `historyEventWhenFinished` can fire an Ideology history event; VFE Factory's "Mince human corpses" fires
  `ButcheredHuman`. Kimoju's mod removes it.

## The stock neutroamine bill already took human meat

`VFEFactory_SynthesizeNeutroamine` takes 20 of the `MeatRaw` category plus 3 chemfuel and gives 5 neutroamine
in 4800 ticks, with no exclusion list. Human meat (`Meat_Human`, generated by the game from the Human race,
meat market value 0.8) is in `MeatRaw`: vanilla meal recipes have to exclude it from the same category by
name. Nothing in VFE Factory filters human meat except the mincer's output. So, by the code, a hopper with
human meat ticked fed the stock bill before this mod existed; only its text ("rendered animal fat") said
otherwise. **Not tested in game.**

## Drug lab recipes as factory bills (28 Oct 2026)

**Scan.** All 300 active mods and the game were parsed the way RimWorld 1.7 loads them (LoadFolders.xml
version blocks with fallback to the newest lower block, ParentName inheritance). A recipe counts if it lists
the drug lab in `recipeUsers`, if a ThingDef `recipeMaker` does, or if a patch adds it to the lab (only
Luciferium Expansion does that). Result: **86 recipes from 17 sources**; VFE Factory automates 10 on its
medicine granulator and LE Ammo one more (FSX, on the refinery); **75 had no factory bill**, and no active
mod adds granulator bills. Two other benches copy the lab's recipes (VFE Production's electric lab, VGE's
compact lab) and need nothing. No DLC adds a drug lab recipe.

**Machine mechanics** (decompiled VFE Factory and Pipe System, `.local/decompiled/`): the granulator's bills
are hand-written `PipeSystem.ProcessDef`s; VFE Factory generates processes only for the autoloom, the
machining bay and the fish farm, from `recipeMaker`, never from RecipeDefs. `researchPrerequisites` hides a
process from the Add menu until done. The comp ticks every 250 ticks. Output: a hopper-only process spawns on
the interaction cell only when a factory hopper whose filter accepts the first result is there, and waits
("output hopper incorrect" or "full") otherwise; **but** with `maxOutputCount` 0 a failed placement still
resets the process (output lost), and with a cap above `stackLimit - count` the `TryAbsorbStack` return is
ignored, so a batch that overfills the stack loses the surplus. Hence the cap rule. Power is the building's
comp only, drawn in full even when idle (`CompPowerTrader_Overclocked`); no per-process power, skill or
count field exists. Stack limits that bind: `DrugBase` 150 (not 400), medicine 25, hemogen pack 10,
vaccines and antiserums 15, pathogen samples 1.

**Stock value rule**, verified on every stock drug bill (flake 250 work -> 4000 ticks for 4; yayo 350 ->
5600; go-juice 600 -> 9600; wake-up 900 -> 14400; medicine 700 -> 11200; penoxycyline and joints batch 1):
ticks = lab work x 4 x batch, inputs x batch.

**Values.** Every bill's inputs, output, ticks and research are in the mod README; source files, read 28 Oct
2026: Medicines+ `1.6/Defs/Drugs/*.xml` (recipeMaker, WorkToMake; `Aniracetam` inherits no bulk recipe or
work skill, `SSalts` has no research); Deep Pills `1.6/Mods/MedicinesPlus/Defs/RecipeDefs/Infusions.xml`
(the x8 recipes; the x1 recipes use a products form that may not load); Biological Warfare
`1.6/Defs/CombatDiseases/<Disease>.xml` with base cost lists in `Bases/` (vaccine: 8 neutroamine, 1
penoxycyline, 25 pathogen, 2600 work; antiserum: 6 neutroamine, 1 glitterworld medicine, 25 pathogen, 1800;
pills: 6 neutroamine, 1 sample, 600; seven diseases, Infection has no operation research); CE
`Defs/Drugs/Psychite_Psycho.xml` (16 leaves, 600 work, no research); Rimatomics `1.7/Defs/Drug/Iodine.xml`
(3 neutroamine, 500, PenoxycylineProduction); Hemofungus `1.4/Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_ExtractHemogen.xml`
(60 -> 4, 1600, Deathrest); Complementary Odyssey `Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_Production.xml` (1 vacdew + 5
steel or vacstone blocks, 600, DrugProduction); Glitterworld Medicine `Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_Production.xml`
(5 synthread, 1 component, 4 plasteel, 1 medicine, 1800, MedicineUltratechProduction); Farmable Neutroamine
`1.6/Defs/ThingDefs_Items/Items_Resource_Manufactured.xml` (5 leaves -> 1, 250, NeutroaleafRefining);
Passive Exoskeletons `1.7/Defs/Recipe/Recipe_materials.xml` (75 chemfuel + 8 plasteel -> 25, 6000; its
x100 recipe already names the refinery as a recipe user, which does nothing on a processor building);
Simple Washing Machine `Defs/RecipeDefs/DetergentRecipes.xml` (25 chemfuel + 1 or 3 neutroamine -> 3, 1500
or 2000, SterileMaterials).

**Deep Pills on 1.7.** Its `loadFolders.xml` has only a v1.6 block, with the Medicines+ payload in an
`IfModActive` subfolder. The owner sees the infusion bills at the drug lab on 1.7 (28 Oct 2026), so the
fallback loads it; our infusion Defs carry `MayRequire="ODs.DeepPills"` inside the Medicines+ folder.

**Not ported, and why:** Luciferium Expansion (owner); Biological Warfare's seven pathogen samples
(1 pathogen -> 1 sample, 200 work: trivial, and samples stack to 1), the disease sampler (a utility item),
flesh-breaker (left out of the scope question; trivial to add); the rumsuit (VFE Pirates) and medic bag
(VAE Accessories), apparel with quality; Deep Pills' x1 infusions (the x8 ones cover them); FSX (LE Ammo
covers it on the refinery).

**Checks.** `scripts/check-factory-patches.py` (added in this change) loads the game and the active mods as
RimWorld would, then checks every patch xpath matches one node, every Def name a ProcessDef uses exists,
every hopper-only bill's cap is within its stack limit, and that defined and added bills match. Run against
the owner's installation on 28 Oct 2026: 299 active mods, 35589 Def names, 45 ProcessDefs, 0 errors. It
found the missing cap on the 0.1.0 human-meat bill. An offline check is not an in-game test.

## Why the 0.3.0 bills never reached the machines (29 Oct 2026)

**Owner's report:** the new bills showed in RimDex but not in any machine's Add process menu.

**Cause, read in RimWorld 1.7's code** (`Verse.ModContentPack.InitLoadFolders` and
`Verse.DirectXmlLoader.XmlAssetsInModFolder`, decompiled from the installed `Assembly-CSharp.dll`; output kept
in the ignored `.local/`): the game walks a mod's load folders from the last one `LoadFolders.xml` lists to the
first and keys every Defs and Patches file by its path **inside its load folder**. The first file to claim a
path loads; every other file with that path is skipped, with no log line. 0.3.0 had seven
`Patches/MedicineGranulator.xml`, two `Patches/BiofuelRefinery.xml`, and a
`Patches/NeutroamineSynthesizer.xml` in both `Common` and `Mods/FarmableNeutroamine`. The bill Defs had
unique file names, so they all loaded (hence RimDex), but only the last folder's patch for each machine ran.

**Effect with the owner's mods:** 4 of 45 bills reached a machine (glitterworld medicine, still hidden by
its research; both detergents; leaf neutroamine). The human-meat bill, its stock-bill exclusion and the
stock bill's corrected text were dropped too; queued human-meat bills kept running because their Def loaded.

**What ruled out the alternatives first:** the owner's Player.log had no patch failure from this mod; Pipe
System's Add menu (1.6 and 1.7 builds, identical here) lists the machine's `processes` and hides only bills
with unfinished research; the save's research covered 25 of the 44 new bills (all of Medicines+, the
infusions, psycho, vacsop, leaf neutroamine, polyamide and the detergents); no other active mod's XML or DLL
replaces the granulator's, synthesizer's or refinery's process list.

**Why the check missed it:** `check-factory-patches.py` loaded every file in every active folder. Both
checks now share `files_the_game_loads` in `check-mod-layout.py`. Run against the 0.3.0 commit's files, the
factory checker reports the 9 skipped patch files and 41 bills on no machine; the layout check refuses a
shared path without needing the game.

**Separate finding, repaired in 0.3.2** (`Mods/VanillaChemfuelExpanded/Patches/VFEFactoryRepair_VanillaChemfuelExpanded.xml`;
`PatchOperationSequence` stopping at its first failed step confirmed in the 1.7 assembly): with Combat Extended, Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded and Vanilla Factions
Expanded - Deserters all active, VFE Factory's own `VanillaChemfuelExpandedPatch.xml` fails at its
armour-piercing shell step, because Combat Extended's VFE Factory patch removes that bill first. A failed step ends
its PatchOperationSequence, so the two steps after it never run: `considerBuildingCompResource` on
`VFEFactory_AstrofuelFromChemfuel`, and the factory-hopper flag on Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded's chemfuel tap.
Seen in the owner's log (29 Oct 2026); the consequence is read from the XML, not seen in game.

## How machines take from hoppers, and the deep factory hopper (29 Oct 2026)

Code read in Pipe System 1.7 and VFE Factory, not tested in game:

- **Machines.** While a bill waits, `Process.CheckInputSlots` visits each `autoInputSlots` cell (rotated with
  the machine), skips it unless a building there carries `FactoryHopperExtension` (for hopper-only bills),
  and offers **every item on the cell** to every ingredient. `ThingAndResourceOwner.AddFromThing` takes only
  what that ingredient still needs (splitting the stack) and keeps a running count inside the machine; the
  batch starts when every count is full. So an input cell is not tied to an ingredient.
- **Why a shared ordinary hopper clogs.** It holds one stack. If the stack on it is something the machine
  already has enough of, nothing else can land there, and the next ingredient waits behind it on the belt.
- **Belts.** Pushing into storage, a belt counts the items on each cell against
  `GetMaxItemsAllowedInCell` (the building's `maxItemsInCell`) and also allows merging into a stack of the
  same item with room; pulling, it takes the first item stack it finds, whole.
- **Output.** A finished batch merges into the first matching stack on the output cell, and waits when that
  stack is above the bill's cap; items made with a stuff (`useFirstIngredientAsOutputStuff`, such as the
  machining bay's weapons) are placed as new things instead, so a multi-stack output cell holds several.
  **Corrected later on 29 Oct 2026:** most machining bay weapons are *not* made with a stuff (VFE sets the flag
  only when the item has a stuff cost), and for them the merge loses the new item; see the next section.
- **VFE's hopper Def** (`VFEFactory_FactoryHopper`) sets `maxItemsInCell` 1 and uses
  `Building_FactoryHopper`, which replaces the Def's graphic with three fixed textures (plain, input,
  output); only the autofarmer's placement preview names that Def directly.
- **The deep hopper** (`PhobosFactory_DeepFactoryHopper`) inherits that Def through a Name added by
  `PatchOperationAttributeAdd` (inheritance resolves after patches), overriding `maxItemsInCell` (4), cost
  and work. Because the class draws fixed textures, it looks the same as the ordinary hopper. A custom look
  would need a small C# subclass and new art: not done.

## The fabrication bay and the lost-item fix (29 Oct 2026)

The owner asked why bionic replacements had no VFE Factory support while bone replacements did. Code read in
the decompiled 1.7 assemblies, not tested in game. Sources, by author: Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory by
Oskar Potocki, Sarg Bjornson and Taranchuk ([Workshop 3686924415](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3686924415));
Pipe System, part of Vanilla Expanded Framework by the Vanilla Expanded team
([GitHub](https://github.com/Vanilla-Expanded/VanillaExpandedFramework)); RimWorld 1.7 by Ludeon Studios;
Dubs Rimatomics by Dubwise ([Workshop 1127530465](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1127530465));
Medical System Expansion 2 by JonM0 ([Workshop 2056706586](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2056706586)).
Decompiled output stays in the ignored `.local/decompiled/`; what follows is a summary in our own words.

**Why bionics had no bills.** VFE Factory's machining bills are generated, not written: a Harmony prefix on
`DefGenerator.GenerateImpliedDefs_PreResolve` makes one bill on the automated machining bay for every ThingDef
whose `recipeMaker.recipeUsers` contains `TableMachining` (and whose defName lacks "Shell"), with the cost
list as ingredients, steel for a metallic stuff cost, textiles for fabric, `WorkToMake` x 4 ticks, the
item's research plus Complex factories, and `maxOutputCount` 1 from its template. Medical System Expansion 2's
steel bones (research `BoneProstheticsSimple`, "bone replacements") are made at the machining table; bionics
(vanilla research `Bionics`, "bionic replacements") at the fabrication bench, which VFE never reads.

**What no machine makes, on the owner's list** (a scan of the active mods' Def files with inheritance, before
other mods' patches): 141 fabrication bench items, 90 smithy items (16 already on the machining bay), 71 DMS
printer and artifuscle items (16 already), 27 Rimatomics machining table items, 23 bioferrite shaper items (4
already), and smaller sets (Alpha Genes' forge, Customize Weapon modules, VVE car parts, alchemy, serums). The
automated assembler already makes components and advanced components.

**The lost-item bug.** `Process.TrySpawnAtCell`, for a bill not made from stuff, finds the first thing on
the output cell with the same Def that `CanStackWith` the new one, and, unless that stack is above
`maxOutputCount`, calls `TryAbsorbStack` and reports success whatever it returns. The game's
`Thing.CanStackWith` checks Def, stuff, relic status and comps (quality) but **not the stack limit**, and
`TryAbsorbStackNumToTake` takes `min(count, stackLimit - stackCount)`. So with a gun (stack limit 1) already
in the output hopper and VFE's limit of 1, the new gun absorbs 0, is never spawned, and is gone with its
ingredients. The same path loses the part of any batch that overflows a nearly full stack when the limit
is above stack limit minus batch, or 0. The safe limit is `stackLimit - batch`, or any value below 1 (0.5)
when that is under 1, so any identical item makes the machine wait (`Phobos.Factory.Rules.OutputCapRule`,
unit-tested). Steam's comment pages were rate-limited, so no player report was checked; the finding rests on
the code.

**Other things the design rests on:**

- With `useFirstIngredientAsOutputStuff`, Pipe System makes the item from `cachedIngredients.Last()`: the
  **last** ingredient taken, not the first. An item made of a stuff plus parts could get a component as its
  material (`ThingMaker` then logs an error and picks a default). Such items are skipped.
- `ProcessDef.ShowProcess()` returns true and is not virtual; the Add process menu
  (`CompAdvancedResourceProcessor.ProcessesOptions`) checks only `researchPrerequisites`. Its options carry
  the bill's `labelOverride` as their label; the item icon is in a private field.
- Dubs Rimatomics gates its parts with its own research by a Harmony postfix on `RecipeDef.AvailableNow`
  (`Harmony_RecipeDef_AvailableNow`), so asking the implied `Make_<item>` recipe honours it, as well as
  memes, faction tags and research.
- `considerBuildingCompResource` draws an ingredient from the building's first `CompResource` when the
  ingredient is that pipe network's first `linkToRefuelables` thing (chemfuel for VCHE's network).
- RimWorld generates the `Make_<item>` recipes inside `GenerateImpliedDefs_PreResolve`, before a postfix runs.
- The game and Pipe System members the assembly calls were found in the 1.7 assemblies with the same
  signatures as in the 1.6 reference assemblies it builds against. VFE Factory runs its 1.6 assembly on 1.7
  the same way.

**What 0.5.0 does.** A `BenchBillTemplateDef` per bench (fabrication bench; bioferrite shaper with Anomaly;
Rimatomics machining table in `Mods/Rimatomics`) names the automated fabrication bay. A postfix on
`GenerateImpliedDefs_PreResolve` makes one bill per item: cost as ingredients (one stuff mapped as VFE does,
or the only fitting stuff), recipe `workAmount` or `WorkToMake` x 4, product count from the recipe, research
of the template plus the item, the safe output limit, piped ingredients where the bay's pipe supplies them,
and a `GeneratedBillExtension` pointing at `Make_<item>`. It skips items any ProcessDef already makes,
buildings, and stuff-plus-parts items, and logs one summary line. A postfix on `ProcessesOptions` removes
options of generated bills whose recipe is not `AvailableNow`, matched by label (kept unique per machine),
and shows a disabled "no bills yet" option if nothing is left. A `SafeOutputLimitsExtension` patched onto
VFE's machining bay (inherited by the fabrication bay) has every bill's limit lowered to the safe one at load,
except bills with a custom result worker (the mechanoid shredding bills, whose steel follows body size).
(Replaced the same day in 0.5.1 by the output guard below.)

**Not done, and why.** Smithies, DMS printers and the smaller benches were offered and not chosen. The bay
looks identical to the machining bay (new art waits for the owner's go-ahead on GPU work). Stuff-plus-parts
items would need a change in Pipe System or a Harmony patch on its output step.

## The output guard for every factory machine (0.5.1, 29 Oct 2026)

The owner asked whether the lost-item bug predated our changes (it did: it is VFE Factory's and Pipe
System's, and no version before 0.5.0 touched the machining bay), then asked to extend the fix to every
affected machine. **0.5.0's limit-lowering marker and `OutputCapRule` are replaced** by the guard below.

**Scale (read-only scan, the owner's 300 active mods).** Of 134 XML ProcessDefs that output only to factory
hoppers, 59 were safe (including all of ours), 51 had a fixed batch and an unsafe limit, and 23 a batch
decided by a result worker at run time; one output Def was not found. Most VFE Factory limits **equal the
stack limit** (chemfuel 150, steel 75, shells 25). Pipe System waits only while the stack is *above* the
limit, so with limit = stack limit a full stack never makes it wait: `TryAbsorbStack` then takes nothing and
**every later batch is lost**. Bills with no limit (VFE's stock neutroamine bill, Kimoju's drill and
neutroamine bills) merge without any check. Runtime-generated bills (VFE's machining bay, limit 1; Munitions
Industries' press) were not in the XML count.

**Why a guard and not lower limits.** A fixed limit cannot protect the 23 variable bills (mincing corpses by
body size, ovens by nutrition, smelting by the item smelted, shredding by mechanoid size), and per-machine
markers would have to follow every mod's bills. The loss happens at one point, so the fix is there.

**What it does.** A Harmony prefix on `PipeSystem.Process.TrySpawnAtCell(Result, IntVec3, Map, out Thing)`
(identical in the 1.6 and 1.7 builds) asks `OutputGuard.WouldLoseBatch` first. For hopper-only bills not
made from stuff, it mirrors the step: the same `GetOutput` and `GetCount`, wrapped in `Rand.PushState` and
`PopState` so random workers (the drill's metal, the fish farm's 1 to 3) give Pipe System the same answer
straight after; a cheap test for any same-Def stack without room; then a stand-in item given Pipe System's
own quality and ingredients (its private `ApplyIngredientsAndQuality`, reached through `AccessTools`), and
the first thing on the cell that `CanStackWith` it. If the batch would not fit there, the prefix returns
false from the step, and for a bill with a limit sets `outputFactoryHopperTooFull` and `tickLeft = 1`, which
is what Pipe System does above the limit. With no limit, Pipe System's caller then tries the cells around the
machine, its own fallback. The prefix skips the original only in that case; the reason is recorded in the
patch class (there is no hook between choosing the stack and losing the items, and a transpiler would break
on any edit to the method). Each patch class is now applied separately, so a failed guard leaves the bills
working.

**Behaviour that changes for the player:** machines that silently consumed inputs at a full output hopper now
stop and say the hopper is too full. The fabrication bay's generated bills take `min(75, stack limit)` as
their limit (so a gun's reads "max 1 units"); the machining bay keeps VFE's 1.

## Belts that take from any storage (0.6.0, 29 Oct 2026)

The owner asked for one generic way for VFE Factory's belts to pull from any container, instead of a patch per
building and mod (the corpse fridge in 0.2.0, the chemfuel taps in 0.3.2), and stressed the cost to run.

**What the code says (read 29 Oct 2026 in the decompiled VFE Factory, Pipe System 1.6 and 1.7, Adaptive Storage
Framework and Rimefeller assemblies; not tested in game).**

- `Building_Conveyor.PeriodicCacheCheck` runs about once a second per belt (`(TicksGame + thingIDNumber) % 60`)
  and sets the private `hasAdjacentHopper` when a neighbouring `Building_Storage` is VFE's `Building_FactoryHopper`
  or carries `FactoryHopperExtension`. Only then does `ProcessMovement` call `TryPullItem` on each idle tick,
  which tries `TryPullFromDir` behind, then clockwise, then counter-clockwise. That method takes the first
  spawned Item on the storage's slot cells, whole stack, and puts it on the belt. Nothing else sets the flag.
- `Building_Conveyor.ReceiveBypass(Thing)` (public) is VFE's own hand-over: it takes the item from any holder,
  adds it to the belt and sets the belt moving. `HasSpace()` is public too.
- `Process.InputFactoryHopperDetected(IntVec3)` (public, Pipe System) decides whether a hopper-only bill reads
  an input cell, by the same Def flag. Machines poll inputs every 100 ticks (`CompTickInterval`). Output uses a
  separate `FactoryHopperDetected()`, left alone.
- The flag is per Def, so no XML or Def change can make it depend on one building: a per-building switch needs
  code at those two decision points.
- Adaptive Storage's `AdaptiveStorage.ThingClass` derives from `Building_Storage` and spawns its stored items on
  the map. Rimefeller's "refinery unloading bay" (`RefineryLoadingBay`, parent `ChemfuelHopperBase`) is a plain
  `Building_Storage`, and `CompRefinery` places its products on the bay's cell with `GenPlace.TryPlaceThing`.
  Both can therefore feed belts. (The 28 Oct conveyor record said a Rimefeller bay is not `Building_Storage`;
  that was wrong, and a correction note is added there.)
- Kimoju's "VFE Factories: Buffs and tweaks" patches only the autofarmer and the fish farm, not belts.

**Survey (read-only, the owner's 300 active mods, by each ThingDef's resolved `thingClass`).** 105 Adaptive
Storage buildings (Warehouse Storage, the [sbz] mods, Cargo Holds, Adaptive Ideology Storage, [kyzy] Ship Wall
Enhanced), 30 plain `Building_Storage` (vanilla shelves and hopper, VFE furniture, Rimefeller bays, VCHE hoppers
and siphons, DMS, VGE, VNPE and others), and a scatter of single classes. The containers that are not
`Building_Storage` hold their contents in an inner container rather than on the map: graves and sarcophagi,
ancient and DMS crates, bookcases, growth vats, biosculpter pods, outfit stands, gibbet cages. Belts should not
empty those, and VFE's pull could not read them anyway.

**The three designs put to the owner.** Flag every storage type at load (no running cost, no Harmony, but any
belt beside any shelf drains it, in an existing colony too, which the project's rule against moving the
player's things forbids without consent); a switch per storage (chosen); a loader belt that pulls from
whatever is behind it (no VFE patches, but identical-looking until it has art, and no help for input cells).

**What 0.6.0 does.**

- `BeltAccessSetup.AddSwitches`, called from the existing postfix on `DefGenerator.GenerateImpliedDefs_PreResolve`
  (so the game resolves and checks the comp like XML), adds `CompProperties_BeltAccess` to every ThingDef whose
  `thingClass` is a `Building_Storage`, skipping Defs that already carry `FactoryHopperExtension`. It logs the
  count.
- `CompBeltAccess` holds one saved bool (`PhobosFactory_BeltsMayTake`, default off), shows a `Command_Toggle` on
  player-owned storage and an inspect line while on. The toggle delegates to `BeltAccess.SetBeltsMayTake`, which
  sets it and sends one silent message (no look target, so the game merges a multi-select into one).
- A postfix on `PeriodicCacheCheck` also sets `hasAdjacentHopper` when switched-on storage is behind or at
  either side (three `GetFirstThing<Building_Storage>` lookups, once a second, only while the flag is false).
  VFE recomputes the flag from scratch each time, so switching off takes effect within a second.
- A postfix on `TryPullFromDir`, only when VFE's own pull found nothing on that side: every 10 ticks
  (`IsHashIntervalTick`, VFE's own rhythm for items on a belt cell) it takes the first Item on the storage's
  cached slot cells (`AllSlotCellsList`, no allocation) with `SplitOff` and hands it over with `ReceiveBypass`.
  Hoppers keep priority, and the order of sides stays VFE's.
- A postfix on `InputFactoryHopperDetected` also accepts switched-on storage on the cell; Pipe System then reads
  every item there, as for a hopper.
- All three are postfixes. Each patch class is applied on its own, so a VFE rename switches off only that part.
  The assembly now references VFE Factory's installed `VanillaFurnitureExpandedFactory.dll` with
  `Private=false`; VFE Factory publishes no licence (its GitHub repository and Workshop folder checked
  29 Oct 2026), so nothing of it is copied ([third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)).

**Cost (estimate from the code, not measured).** Switched-off storage adds nothing while the game runs.
Belts add the neighbour check about once a second each; the storage scan runs only beside switched-on storage
and only every 10 ticks, and an idle belt beside an empty hopper already does more work than that every tick in
stock VFE. The owner runs Dubs Performance Analyzer and can measure it in play.

**Behaviour to watch in play.** A belt beside switched-on storage takes everything, forbidden items included and
whatever its filter says (the hopper rule). A belt that delivers back into storage it takes from loops for ever.
Pulling from storage runs at most once per 10 ticks per belt, a little slower than from a hopper.

**0.6.1 (30 Oct 2026): only the belt behind.** The owner's first test (29 Oct 2026, RimWorld 1.7) showed belts
taking stacks from a switched-on Adaptive Storage fridge, and also belts that merely ran along its sides: a
line wrapped around the fridge emptied it. 0.6.0 had copied the hopper rule (behind and both sides), which
suits a hopper placed to feed one belt but not storage that belts pass. Now `BeltAccess.HasStorageToTakeFrom`
looks only at the cell behind the belt (`Rotation.Opposite`; `TryPullFromDir`'s argument is the direction
from the belt to the neighbour, read in `TryPullItem`), and `TryTakeFrom` returns at once for any other side.
The neighbour check falls from three lookups to one. Factory hoppers and Def-flagged storage keep VFE's own
rule. The 0.6.0 notes also said a hopper beside the same belt goes first; that holds only per side. VFE tries
behind first, so switched-on storage behind a belt goes before a hopper at its side.

## Ideas for later additions (agent suggestions, not decided)

- **Machines that output into switched-on storage** (Pipe System's `FactoryHopperDetected`): would let a
  multi-stack shelf take a machine's output, but the output step merges into the first matching stack only, so
  it needs the 0.5.1 guard's reasoning applied to several stacks first.
- **The corpse fridge on the switch** instead of its always-on flag, for consistency, if the owner prefers it.
  Since 0.6.1 it would also stop feeding belts that only run past it.

- **Flesh-breaker** (Biological Warfare, 2 neutroamine -> 1, 1000 work) and **Luciferium Expansion's** seven
  products, if the owner changes their mind: same shape as the 0.3.0 bills.
- **Hemogen packs from human corpses** on the automated mincer: pure gameplay (corpse blood is not
  transfusable), so an owner decision and labelled as such.

- **Twisted meat (Anomaly)** as a neutroamine feedstock, with its own bill and yield, the way Kimoju treats
  insect meat. Entity flesh is the one meat players rarely want to eat.
- **Rendered fat as an intermediate item** (tallow): corpses to the mincer, meat to a renderer, tallow to the
  synthesizer, soap (Dubs Bad Hygiene) or candles. A pathways design in the owner's taste; a bigger slice.
- **Toxic wastepacks** from drug synthesis (Biotech): Pipe System has `wastePackToProduce`, so a batch could
  leave a little waste. Honest chemistry, but a nerf, so an owner decision.
- **A 1.7 fallback for Kimoju's bills**: that mod declares 1.6 only. If it breaks on 1.7, this mod could carry
  insect and fish bills of its own, switched on only when Kimoju's mod is absent.
- **A mod setting for the yield** needs C#; not worth a DLL for one number while the value is being tuned.
  (Since 0.5.0 the mod has an assembly, so a settings page would now be cheap.)
- **Smithy and DMS printer bills** (0.5.0 survey): one bench template each, on the fabrication bay or the
  machining bay. Not chosen on 29 Oct 2026.
- **A look of its own for the fabrication bay**: new art (owner's go-ahead on GPU work first).
