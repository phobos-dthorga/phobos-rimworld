# Farming batch two's pilots: run and review (20 Nov 2026)

**What was bought.** Ten images, two attempts each of five north-facing bodies, from the OpenAI image API (gpt-image-2, **high
quality**, at about twice each body's drawn size: the owner's lesson of 20 Nov 2026 on fine detail), at the owner's go of 20 Nov 2026
("all pilots in one batch") with a US$2.90 cap. Dry run first: at most US$2.80. Spent: about **US$1.94**. Request:
[farming-batch2-pilots.json](farming-batch2-pilots.json); design: [farming batch two](../../../docs/development/farming-batch-two.md).

**Reference, our own.** Image 1, the Thane T130's standard body the owner kept on 20 Nov 2026
([its record](tractor-pilot-record.md)). No game or other author's art was uploaded.

**Review (agent, each at full size).**

| Body | Attempt 1 | Attempt 2 | Agent's lean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thane T60 | Short bonnet, small cab roof with one beacon, linkage folded with its drawbar plate | The same build, fuller rear tyres, cleaner mudguards | 2 |
| Thane T400, drawn whole | Dual wheels on both halves, a clear pivot with a white gap; mirrors on thin arms | Cleaner; a tidy rear half and fuel deck; the same clear pivot | 2 |
| Sillion plough | Mouldboards on the right, depth wheel left | Mouldboards on the left, a longer beam: the mirror of 1 | either |
| Tillman cultivator | Three even rows of tines (drawn as side-on S-shapes, a stylisation) and a crumbler roller | Uneven tine spacing, one doubled tine top right | 1 |
| Coulter seed drill | **Fault:** the discs and the harrow float apart from the body | Discs on a frame bar under the hopper; only the harrow row floats a little | 2 |

**Decisions (owner, 20 Nov 2026).**

- **Thane T60 and Thane T400: keep both attempts of each**, as two models under one Architect button with slightly different figures,
  as the T130's two are.
- **Sillion plough: both, as one reversible (turnover) plough** (the owner's idea, "make it rotatable in some fashion", confirmed by
  card): real large ploughs roll over on the headstock at each headland so every furrow is thrown the same way; attempt 1 is one side
  and attempt 2 the other, swapped as the field run turns it between rows.
- **Tillman cultivator: both attempts, as two models under one Architect button** (the owner, 20 Nov 2026: "like the first answer",
  the T60's), with slightly different figures. Attempt 2's doubled tine (top right) is to be cleaned at export.
- **Coulter seed drill: attempt 2.**

Picks on `art-masters` (`vehicles/farming/`): tractor-t60-body-north-1 and -2, tractor-t400-body-north-1 and -2, plough-body-north-1
and -2, cultivator-body-north-1 and -2, seed-drill-body-north-2. Kept on `art-references`: seed-drill-body-north-1 (the floating discs),
not fugly.
