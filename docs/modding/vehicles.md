# D'thorga Vehicles for modders

How your own mod can give its vehicles what D'thorga Vehicles gives its own and other mods' vehicles. Players start at [the mod's
README](../../mods/PhobosVehicles/README.md) and [the player guide](../player-guide/working-vehicles.md).

## Loadouts for your vehicles

A loadout is an XML file that names a vehicle and the systems and figures it gets. D'thorga Vehicles ships one for each of its own
vehicles and for the other mods' vehicles it knows, under its `Mods/` folders, with every figure's source written beside it. The
Def types are D'thorga Framework's, so **write your own loadout in your own mod**, needing only D'thorga Framework: [vehicle
systems for modders](../vehicle-systems-modding.md), from [a loadout for your
vehicle](../vehicle-systems-modding.md#a-loadout-for-your-vehicle) to [bulk tanks](../vehicle-systems-modding.md#bulk-tanks),
[armour by class](../vehicle-systems-modding.md#armour-by-class) and [gathering
runs](../vehicle-systems-modding.md#gathering-runs).
