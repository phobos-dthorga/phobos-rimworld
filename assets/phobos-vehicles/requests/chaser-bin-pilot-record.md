from the OpenAI image API (gpt-image-2, medium quality, 1024 x 2048), at the owner's go of 20 Nov 2026 with a US$0.40 cap. Dry run

**Reference, our own.** Image 1, the combine harvester's body the owner picked on 19 Nov 2026 (attempt 1), for style and palette only.

**Review (agent, at about native size).** All three are straight down with no tilt or visible side face, on white, with no text,
grain, crew, tractor or damage. All three have the planned layout: an A-frame drawbar with a towing eye at the top, one deep hopper
whose walls slope to a slot along the middle, the auger folded along the left rim, one pair of big lugged tyres a little behind the
middle, and a rear panel with a ladder.
