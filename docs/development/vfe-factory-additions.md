# VFE Factory additions: research and decisions (28 Oct 2026)

What was read, what was decided and by whom, for the mod `mods/PhobosFactoryAdditions/` (D'thorga Factory
Additions). The owner's intent (28 Oct 2026): a series of modifications to Vanilla Furniture Expanded -
Factory, starting with human meat as a neutroamine feedstock. Dated record; the mod's README and changelog
carry current behaviour.

## How a factory bill works (code read, not tested in game)

Read on 28 Oct 2026 from the decompiled Pipe System (Vanilla Expanded Framework) and VFE Factory assemblies in
the ignored `.local/decompiled/`, and from VFE Factory's installed XML (Workshop item 3686924415).

- A factory bill is a `PipeSystem.ProcessDef`. A building offers the bills listed in its
  `CompProperties_AdvancedResourceProcessor` `processes` list; other mods add to that list with a
  `PatchOperationAdd` on the comp's xpath.
- An ingredient names either one `thing` or a `thingCategory`. A category ingredient accepts anything
  `IsWithinCategory` says is in it, minus the Defs on its `disallowedThingDefs` list (`Process.CheckInputSlots`).
  The owner's earlier finding stands: the factory's own grab ignores the hopper's "allow taking off" state.
- `ticks` is the batch time; `priorityInBillList` orders the Add process menu (stock 0, Kimoju's insect 1,
  our human meat 2, Kimoju's fish 11).
- `considerBuildingCompResource` lets a bill draw a piped resource from the building's own `CompResource`;
  VFE Factory sets it on the stock neutroamine bill only while Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded is active, by
  `PatchOperationFindMod` on the mod's display name.
- `historyEventWhenFinished` can fire an Ideology history event; VFE Factory's "Mince human corpses" fires
  `ButcheredHuman`. Kimoju's mod removes it.

## The stock neutroamine bill already took human meat

`VFEFactory_SynthesizeNeutroamine` takes 20 of the `MeatRaw` category plus 3 chemfuel and gives 5 neutroamine
in 4800 ticks, with no exclusion list. Human meat (`Meat_Human`, generated by the game from the Human race,
meat market value 0.8) is in `MeatRaw`: vanilla meal recipes have to exclude it from the same category by
name. Nothing in VFE Factory filters human meat except the mincer's output. So, by the code, a hopper with
human meat ticked fed the stock bill before this mod existed; only its text ("rendered animal fat") said
otherwise. **Not tested in game.**

| ...from fish meat | Kimoju, with Odyssey | 4 fish + 3 chemfuel | 5 | 4800 | n/a (whole fish) |

The human-meat yield sits between the stock and insect bills. The reasoning (a human carcass is far fattier
than lean game, and the lore renders fat) and its sources are in the mod README; the figure is authored.

## Ideas for later additions (agent suggestions, not decided)

- **Twisted meat (Anomaly)** as a neutroamine feedstock, with its own bill and yield, the way Kimoju treats
  insect meat. Entity flesh is the one meat players rarely want to eat.
- **Rendered fat as an intermediate item** (tallow): corpses to the mincer, meat to a renderer, tallow to the
  synthesizer, soap (Dubs Bad Hygiene) or candles. A pathways design in the owner's taste; a bigger slice.
- **Toxic wastepacks** from drug synthesis (Biotech): Pipe System has `wastePackToProduce`, so a batch could
  leave a little waste. Honest chemistry, but a nerf, so an owner decision.
- **A 1.7 fallback for Kimoju's bills**: that mod declares 1.6 only. If it breaks on 1.7, this mod could carry
  insect and fish bills of its own, switched on only when Kimoju's mod is absent.
- **A mod setting for the yield** needs C#; not worth a DLL for one number while the value is being tuned.
