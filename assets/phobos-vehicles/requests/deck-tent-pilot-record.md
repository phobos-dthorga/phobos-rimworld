# The deck tent drawn on the Pintle flatbed: the pilot (22 Nov 2026)

**Why.** The owner, 22 Nov 2026, by card: camp gear fitted to our own vehicles is drawn on them, paid (in-world art), **one pilot first**, the
deck tent on the Pintle flatbed, before deciding on the rest. Never on other authors' vehicles.

**How (paid rule 13).** A crop of the picked body's deck (`assets/phobos-vehicles/references/deck-tent-crops.json`, kept locally as references are; our own art, the only
reference) was sent as an edit: "add a canvas deck tent ... change nothing else". Two attempts from [deck-tent-pilot.json](deck-tent-pilot.json),
OpenAI gpt-image-2, medium; dry run first (at most US$0.20), cap US$0.40, spent about **US$0.06**. Each tent was lifted out with
`scripts/art/sam_lift.py --cpu` ([deck-tent-sam-lift.json](deck-tent-sam-lift.json); scores 0.93 and 0.96, one piece each) and laid back
at the crop's place, then exported at the flatbed's own transform.

**Review (agent).** Laid on the trailer's tinted game picture on soil-coloured ground, at game size and half size: both clean, no white
fringe, inside the rails. Attempt 1 is fuller but lost its guy lines and part of its rear roll; attempt 2 is a little narrower with its guy
lines and rolled door flap whole. The agent suggested **2** and shipped it while the owner's pick is waited for.

**Decision (owner):** waiting: the pick, and whether the rest of the family is worth buying (the Tipper's deck tent and the fold-out camper
on the flatbed, about three overlays; the roof tents and awning rolls on our trucks, about ten more).
