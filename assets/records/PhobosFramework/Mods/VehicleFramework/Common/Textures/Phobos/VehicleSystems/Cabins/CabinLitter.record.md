# CabinLitter

Made on 25 Nov 2026 with OpenAI gpt-image-2 through the image API, at medium quality: **AI-generated**, a casualty litter in a vehicle's walk-in cabin, 1 x 2 drawn at 2 x 2, replacing the base game's hospital bed. Picked by the owner from two attempts (attempt 1) on the Picks page. Exported by `scripts/art/export_sprite.py` at 256 px on its shorter side (1:1) after the background AI had looked at the master, by `assets/vehicle-systems/ship_paid25.py`. The prompt and its references are in `assets/vehicle-systems/requests/paid-2026-11-25.json`; the master and its record go to the `art-masters` branch, the other attempt to `art-references`. The only references uploaded were our own approved art, for style only. No game or other author's art was uploaded or used.
The master is the south facing, the pillow at the top as on the game's own beds (a bed faces from its head to its foot); north and east are it turned by script (180 degrees, and 90 degrees anticlockwise so the pillow is on the left); the game mirrors east for west.
