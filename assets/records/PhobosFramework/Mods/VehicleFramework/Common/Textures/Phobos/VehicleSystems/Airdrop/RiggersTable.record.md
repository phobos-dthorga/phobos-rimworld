# RiggersTable

Made on 25 Nov 2026 with OpenAI gpt-image-2 through the image API, at medium quality: **AI-generated**, the rigger's table, a 4 x 1 work table drawn at (4.5, 1.5), Graphic_Multi with four facings. Picked by the owner from two attempts (attempt 2) on the Picks page (pick `airdrop-riggers-table`). Exported by `scripts/art/export_sprite.py` at 576 x 192 (3:1) after the background AI had looked at the master, by `assets/vehicle-systems/ship_airdrop.py`. The prompt and its references are in `assets/vehicle-systems/requests/airdrop-paid.json`; the master and its record go to the `art-masters` branch, the other attempt to `art-references`. The only references uploaded were our own approved art, for style only. No game or other author's art was uploaded or used.
The north facing is the master, the rigger standing at its bottom edge; east, south and west are it turned by script (90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees and 90 degrees anticlockwise), so the four facings register exactly. The colour masks (`_northm` and the rest, red where the stuff tints, for CutoutComplex) are drawn by script (`table_mask` in the ship script): the steel frame and the wooden top only, never the canopy, pack, paddle, tools or bags on it, and turned with their facings.
