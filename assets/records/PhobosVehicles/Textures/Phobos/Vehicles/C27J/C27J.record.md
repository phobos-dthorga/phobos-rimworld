# C-27J Spartan textures: asset record

Made on 26 Nov 2026: **AI-generated**, both masters picked by the owner on D'thorga Companion's Picks page. The body is Google's Nano
Banana Pro through fal.ai (body try 1, the owner's pick); the propeller is the same model's edge-on try 4, its outline redrawn by script
(the owner's pick "4 outlined"). The only references uploaded were our own: the Osprey's picked body and rotor and our plan drawings
(`assets/phobos-vehicles/draw_c27j_plan.py`). No game or other author's art was uploaded or used; the owner's photograph of a real
propeller was not uploaded, and the redraw worked from the owner's description. Masters and their records are on the `art-masters`
branch (`vehicles/c27j/`); every other attempt and the plan drawings are on `art-references` (`generated/vehicles/c27j/`).

**How they were made** (the requests, every try, the owner's verdicts and the spend:
[c27j-paid-record.md](../../../../../../phobos-vehicles/requests/c27j-paid-record.md)):

- **Exported** by `assets/phobos-vehicles/export_c27j.py` at 120 px a cell (at 128 the 34-cell canvas would pass 4,096 px): the body
  22.7 m nose to tail at 1.175 cells a metre, on a 34 x 27 cell canvas; east and south are the north turned. The background AI looked at
  both masters first, on the processor.
- **Spinners in black by script** before export (the real spinner is black, by the owner's description), so the propeller's own black
  spinner leaves no grey beside it.
- **Paint:** the airframe's grey is the main paint; it has no second colour, so the masks are main paint only. Glass and the spinners
  stay bare; thin seam lines take the paint of the panels around them. Decided by region. Segment Anything's repair was tried on the
  processor (26 Nov 2026) and found 0 visible flecks either way, so the region paint stands.
- **Defensive aids pods** (`C27J_DefensiveAids`, 26 Nov 2026): the owner's pick of the redrawn pod (try 4 of
  `requests/c27j-overlays.json`, Nano Banana Pro through fal, drawn alone on white after the first two were rejected as "a Ring doorbell
  thingy"), cut from its white ground and laid by script on the body's canvas, one on each main-gear fairing, the right the left turned
  over; about 1.4 m long (agent's placement). Shown by the C-27J's defensive aids upgrade.
- **Gun kit barrel** (`C27J_GunKit`, 26 Nov 2026): the owner's pick of the slim barrel (try 1 of `requests/c27j-overlays.json`, Nano
  Banana Pro through fal, drawn alone on white), cut out and set as Vehicle Framework's turret picture: barrel up, its collar at the
  centre of a 4-cell square, 1.6 m to the muzzle (agent's figure). The gun kit's turret, out of the left jump door.
- **Fire-fighting tank pipe** (`C27J_FireTank`, 26 Nov 2026): the owner's pick of the pipe (try 2, the same request), cut out, turned a
  quarter anticlockwise and laid by script with its flange on the left jump door, so it reaches out and bends aft; about 1.4 m long
  (agent's figure). Shown by the C-27J's fire-fighting tank upgrade.
- **Propeller** (`C27J_Propeller`, `C27J_PropellerSpin`): 4.115 m across [P, EASA] at the body's scale, on a 5 x 2.5 cell canvas centred
  on the hub (the blade plane at the spinner's base), for Framework's CompPhobosPropellers. The turning one is made by script: the blades'
  sweep as a see-through blur, densest toward the tips (a turning blade spends longest near the ends of its path seen edge on), the
  spinner kept crisp. `C27J.layout.json` gives where they sit.
- Not yet seen in game.
