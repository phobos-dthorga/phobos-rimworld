# AwningCanvas

Made with ComfyUI, local, FLUX.2 Klein 4B base (Black Forest Labs, Apache 2.0) with our private house style model (house-v1, trained on our own picks), on 21 November 2026: **AI-generated**, one try, picked by the owner the same evening. The prompt is in `assets/vehicle-systems/requests/camp-gear-house.json`; the batch report is `assets/vehicle-systems/generated/run-20261121-2142.md`. Only our own shipped art was used as references, for style; no game or other author's art. The master drew a frame round the canvas, which would show as a seam between rows, so the canvas inside the frame was cut out by script into a new file, `camp-awning-canvas-11-inner.png` (pixels 176 to 592 across, 222 to 1318 down; the master is kept unchanged), and scaled to 128 x 256 (one cell along by two out) with Lanczos. It is drawn see-through over the awning's cells, the fringe away from the vehicle. No background to clear: the canvas fills the picture.
